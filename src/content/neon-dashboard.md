# Neon Dashboard

*A real-time analytics workspace for creative studios.*

## Overview

Neon Dashboard is a collaborative analytics workspace that turns dense
product data into a calm, legible surface. Designed for teams who need to
make fast, confident decisions without fighting their tooling.

## Highlights

- **Live cursors &amp; presence** — see who is looking at what, in real
  time.
- **Composable widgets** — drag, drop, and configure from a library of
  20+ first-class visualizations.
- **Typographic hierarchy** — a calm, type-first reading experience even
  when data is dense.

## Stack

Built with `Next.js`, `TypeScript`, and `D3`. Deployed as a static export
with real-time data pushed over WebSocket.
