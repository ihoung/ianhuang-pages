# About

I design and build digital products at the intersection of **artistry** and
**technology**. From concept sketches to shipping interfaces, I care about
clarity, pace, and the small details that make software feel alive.

## What I do

- **Product design** — end-to-end UX, from problem framing to high-fidelity
  UI and motion.
- **Front-end engineering** — Next.js, React, TypeScript, and design systems
  at scale.
- **Brand &amp; identity** — typography-first identity systems for studios
  and products.

## Philosophy

> "Good design is as little design as possible." — Dieter Rams

I believe the quietest interfaces are often the loudest in impact. Every
pixel, transition, and word should earn its place on the screen.
